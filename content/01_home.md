# Home

**Qyronlab**
*Computation you can check — prepared for the quantum internet.*

Quantum computers will not grow into useful machines one chip at a time. They will be connected — and once they
are, a program running across several of them has to ask the network for entanglement, wait for it, and use it
before it fades. Qyronlab builds the layer that decides what a program asks for, keeps the books on every resource
it uses, and lets an independent party check those books.

We are an independent research lab at an early stage. Everything here describes work that exists, at the size it
actually has.
