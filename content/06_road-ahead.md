# The road ahead

1. Split a small program over several machines and insert the teleportations, checked against an exact simulation.
2. Run the predictions on real quantum hardware: how much a pair loses while it waits, and how much of it can be won
   back, measured — not modelled.
3. Measure the hardware requirements of the room-temperature node with partner laboratories.
4. Bring the choreographer's requests to real network testbeds.
