# How we work

**Two independent arms.** A result counts only when two independent arms agree: our calculation, and a check that
does not share its code — or a partner's own measurement on their own instrument. The tolerance is written down
before the measurement, never adjusted after it.

**Sealed first, revealed after.** Each side first publishes only the fingerprint of its result, and shows the result
itself later. Neither side can lean towards the other's answer, and the fingerprint proves the prediction existed
before the measurement — without giving it away early.

**Step by step.** We do not claim what we have not measured. Each step has its acceptance criteria fixed in advance,
and each step is reported with its limits.

**In writing.** We work best in writing; it keeps a precise, shared record for both sides.
