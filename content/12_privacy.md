**What this site does not do.** The site is a set of static pages. It has no user accounts, no comment forms, no
newsletter, no advertising, and it sets no tracking or analytics cookies. We do not profile visitors.

**Server logs.** The hosting provider [tárhely-szolgáltató neve] records technical access data (IP address, time, page

30 nap].

**E-mail.** When you write to us, we process your name, your e-mail address, and what you write, in order to answer and
to keep a record of the correspondence. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and in documenting

concerns is open, and afterwards for [megőrzési idő, pl. 5 év] as a record of scientific priority. We do not pass it on
to anyone except where the law requires it.

written consent, which can be withdrawn at any time by e-mail.

**Your rights.** You may ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, object to the processing, or
ask for a copy. Write to contact@qyronlab.com. You may also complain to the Hungarian National Authority for Data
Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH, naih.hu).

**Links.** Other websites we link to, including zynko.dev, have their own privacy notices.

**Changes.** We may update this notice; the new version applies from the date shown above.
