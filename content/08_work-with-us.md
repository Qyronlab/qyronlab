# Work with us

We are looking for partners who measure — laboratories and groups with instruments, testbeds or quantum hardware —
and who are curious about a new line of research.

**How a collaboration works with us**
1. **Sealed first.** We write down our prediction and the tolerance it must meet, and send you only its
   fingerprint (a cryptographic hash) — before anything is measured. You cannot see what we expect; we can no longer
   change it.
2. You measure on your own instruments, independently of us, and send us the fingerprint of your result.
3. **Revealed after.** Both sides open their result; anyone can check each one against its fingerprint. If the two
   agree within the tolerance, the result stands for both sides; if not, we have both learned where the model fails.
4. Each side opens a little more as results come in. Everything in writing.

**What you get:** first-hand access to a new line of research, and predictions you can test on equipment you already
have. **What we get:** measurements we cannot make ourselves.

**Where we stand.** We have contacted a research laboratory in Switzerland with a concrete request: a field map of a
small permanent magnet, and a map of the radio-frequency field of a coil, both measured at room temperature. They took
the request seriously, but had no one available to work on it this year. So we keep looking for research groups and
university departments that can give a substantive answer to this question.

We name partners on this page only with their written consent.
