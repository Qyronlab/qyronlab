# Hardware, measured before it is claimed

Network nodes need memory that holds entanglement long enough to be used. We are working towards a compact node
built around a permanent magnet; its memory runs cold, but two of its hardest requirements can be measured at room
temperature, before anything is cooled: the uniformity of the magnetic field and of the radio-frequency field over
the sample. The first question is plain: can those be reached with a permanent magnet of this size at all? We are
arranging those measurements with partners who own the instruments, and we will publish what they show.
